Storm Damage & Emergency Roof Repair in Indianapolis

Wind, hail and fallen limbs can open a roof in minutes. We secure it first to stop water getting in, then document the damage and repair or replace what's needed.

What to do right now

  1. Stay off the roof
  2. Move valuables away from leaks and catch water
  3. Photograph damage you can see safely from the ground
  4. Call (317) 402-9681

What we do

  • Tarp or temporarily secure the roof
  • Inspect every slope, flashing and gutters
  • Photograph and document the damage
  • Give you a written scope for your insurance conversation
  • Repair or replace the roof and siding

Insurance

Storm damage may be covered by your homeowner's or commercial policy, depending on your coverage. We don't promise what an insurer will pay, but we give you clear photos and a written scope for your adjuster.
