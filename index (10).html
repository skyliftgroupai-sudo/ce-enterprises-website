About us

Owner-Operated. Honest. On Site.

C.E. Enterprises Indy Roofing Siding & Restoration LLC is run by owner Chris Epple out of our shop at 2065 S Kitley Ave in Indianapolis. When you call, you talk to the person responsible for your project — and he's the one who shows up to look at it.

We started with roofs and siding — solving water problems and keeping homes weather-tight — and grew into a full general contractor building room additions, custom homes, kitchens and bathrooms. Customers keep mentioning the same things in their reviews: clear communication, clean job sites and no surprises on price.

Frequently Asked Questions

What services does C.E. Enterprises offer?

Roofing and siding, home additions, custom home building, kitchen remodeling, bathroom remodeling and full house renovations — for residential and commercial properties.

What areas do you serve?

We're based at 2065 S Kitley Ave in Indianapolis and serve roughly a 40-mile radius, including Indianapolis, Greenwood, Westfield, Noblesville and Avon.
