Roof repair or roof replacement: how to decide

Most leaks start at one weak point, not across the whole roof. The question is whether the rest of the roof still has years left.

What we findUsually means
One leak at a chimney, wall, skylight or ventRepair: flashing or pipe boot replacement
A few missing or lifted shingles after windRepair, if the surrounding shingles are still flexible and hold granules
Hail bruising, granule loss or cracked shingles on several slopesReplacement, often through an insurance claim
Shingles curling, cracking or bald across most of the roofReplacement: the roof is at the end of its life
Soft or spongy decking in more than one spotReplacement with decking repair
Third repair in a few years, new leaks in new placesReplacement: repeated repairs now cost more than they save

What moves your Greenwood price up or down:

  • Roof size and pitch: steeper roofs need more safety equipment and labor.
  • Layers to tear off: two layers of old shingles cost more to remove than one.
  • Decking: rotted plywood found after tear-off has to be replaced.
  • Flashing, vents and ventilation: done right, they prevent the leaks we're most often called to fix.
  • Material: architectural shingles, metal or specialty roofing.

Small repairs (a flashing detail, a pipe boot, a handful of shingles) cost far less than a replacement. That's why an honest inspection matters before anyone quotes you a full roof.

Hail damage roof repair in Greenwood

Johnson County gets spring and summer storms with wind and hail. Hail damage isn't always obvious from the driveway, and it doesn't take golf-ball hail to hurt a roof.

A 2025 study by the Insurance Institute for Business & Home Safety (IBHS) found that repeated hail under one inch can cause more cumulative granule loss than a single 2-inch hailstone. Shingles that took repeated small hits were about ten times more likely to be damaged by a later severe hailstorm.

Signs of hail damage to look for:

  • Granules collecting in gutters or at downspout outlets
  • Dark spots or "bruises" where granules are knocked off
  • Dents in gutters, downspouts, vents and metal flashing
  • Cracked or split shingles, especially on the side facing the storm
  • Dings on siding, window wraps and outdoor AC units

If you see these, get an inspection before you call your insurance company. Our storm and hail damage guide covers the claim process and your rights under Indiana law.

Emergency roof repair in Greenwood: the first hour

  1. Stay off the roof. Wet shingles and storm debris are dangerous.
  2. Stop the water inside. Move valuables, put buckets under drips, and poke a small hole in a bulging ceiling stain to drain it into a bucket.
  3. Take photos of the ceiling, the attic if it's safe, and any damage you can see from the ground.
  4. Call a roofer to secure the roof. A tarp or temporary patch stops more damage while the permanent repair is planned.
  5. Call your insurance company if the damage came from a storm.

How to choose a roofing company in Greenwood

Greenwood search results are full of lead-selling directories, and storm-chasing crews arrive after every big hailstorm. Before you hire any of the roofers in Greenwood, use this checklist:

  1. A real local address and phone number. You want someone who will still be here when you need warranty work.
  2. Reviews you can read. Check Google and the BBB, not just testimonials on their own website.
  3. An inspection with photos from the roof and the attic, not a guess from the driveway.
  4. A written contract. For contracts signed after June 30, 2017, Indiana law (IC 24-5-11-10.6) requires a Notice of Cancellation form and gives you three business days to cancel, counted from signing or from your insurer's final coverage decision, whichever is later.
  5. No deductible "deals." Indiana law (IC 24-5-11-10.5) prohibits contractors from offering to pay or rebate your insurance deductible to win the job.
  6. Proof of insurance and a clear schedule before work starts.
  7. Who is on site. Will you deal with the owner, or a salesperson who disappears after you sign?
