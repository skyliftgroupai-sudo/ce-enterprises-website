Short answer

A professional roof inspection checks every slope of the roof, the flashing, vents and pipe boots, gutters, decking and the attic, and should end with photos and a plain recommendation: repair, replace or leave it alone. The National Roofing Contractors Association (NRCA) recommends an inspection at least twice a year, in spring and fall, and after any severe storm. Angi lists a national average of about $210, though many roofers give free estimates.

People search "roof inspector near me" for different reasons: a ceiling stain, a storm, a home sale, or a roof that's just getting old. This guide explains what a roof inspection should include, what it costs, how long it takes and when to schedule one in Indianapolis, Greenwood, Avon and Westfield.

You should come away with photos of every problem area and a clear answer. A good inspector will also tell you when nothing needs to be done.

When to get a roof inspection

  • Spring and fall, as NRCA recommends, to catch winter damage and prepare for the next one
  • After hail or high wind. A 2025 IBHS study found repeated hail under one inch can cause more cumulative shingle granule loss than a single 2-inch stone.
  • Before buying or selling a home
  • When you see a ceiling stain, find shingles in the yard or notice granules in the gutters
  • Before an insurance claim, so the damage is documented
  • When the roof is getting old, to plan the replacement instead of reacting to a leak

How much does a roof inspection cost?

Angi lists an average roof inspection cost of about $210, with most between $75 and $600 (2022). Price depends on roof size, access, material and whether you need a formal written report, such as for a home sale or a commercial property.

Free roof inspection or paid inspection?

Many roofing companies, C.E. Enterprises included, look at your roof for free when you're considering a repair or replacement. Two cautions:

  • Ask for photos either way. You should see what they saw.
  • Be wary of door-knockers after storms who find "damage" on every roof and push you to sign before your insurer inspects.

How long does a roof inspection take?

For a typical single-family home, checking the roof and attic usually takes about an hour. Steep, large or complex roofs, tile roofs and commercial buildings take longer, and a written report with photos adds time after the visit.

Commercial roof inspections

Flat and low-slope commercial roofs hide leaks well. Water can travel under the membrane and soak insulation far from the drip. A commercial roof inspection should cover seams, flashing at parapets and curbs, drains and scuppers, ponding, rooftop equipment and any wet insulation.
