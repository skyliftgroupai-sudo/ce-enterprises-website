Short answer

Replace siding when it's warped, cracked, faded across whole walls, loose, or letting moisture into the wall behind it. Repair it when damage is limited to a few panels. Vinyl costs about $3 to $12 per sq ft installed and lasts about 20 to 40 years. Fiber cement costs about $5 to $14 per sq ft and can last up to 50 years with repainting every 10 to 15 years (Angi).

Siding is your home's raincoat. When it fails, water gets into the sheathing and framing behind it, where you can't see it. Here's how Indianapolis homeowners can tell repair from replacement, compare vinyl and fiber cement, budget for siding replacement and choose a siding contractor.

Signs your siding needs replacing

  • Warped, buckled or bulging panels
  • Cracks, holes or chips from hail, mowers or impact
  • Loose or missing pieces after wind
  • Heavy fading across whole walls
  • Soft spots, rot or mold on the wall, or peeling paint inside on exterior walls
  • Gaps at windows, doors and corners where water can get behind it
  • Rising energy bills and drafts, sometimes from damaged siding and missing wrap

What does siding replacement cost in Indianapolis?

Using Angi's national figures, a whole-home siding job averages about $12,205 for vinyl and $14,870 for fiber cement. What changes your price:

  • Wall area and the number of stories
  • Trim, corners, soffit and fascia included in the job
  • Tear-off of old siding
  • Sheathing and house wrap repairs found once the old siding is off
  • Material and profile

Siding repair vs. replacement

Most siding repair in Indianapolis after a storm is panel replacement. Full replacement makes sense when the problem is the whole house, not one wall.

SituationUsually
A few cracked or hail-damaged panels; a matching product is availableRepair: replace the damaged panels

Fading, warping or brittleness across the houseReplacement

How to choose among siding contractors in Indianapolis

  1. Ask what's under the siding. Will they inspect and repair the sheathing and house wrap?
  2. Get the scope in writing: tear-off, wrap, trim, soffit, fascia and cleanup.
