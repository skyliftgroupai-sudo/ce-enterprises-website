Is a metal roof worth it in Indianapolis?

Metal usually makes sense when:

  • You plan to stay 15+ years and don't want to re-roof again
  • You want a low-maintenance roof that sheds snow
  • You're roofing a barn, garage, porch or commercial building where exposed-fastener panels are cost-effective
  • Your HOA allows it

Asphalt shingles usually make sense when:

  • Up-front budget is the main concern
  • You may sell within 10 years
  • You want a roof that's easy to repair and match after storm damage
  • Your HOA requires shingles

Hail: metal vs. shingles

Central Indiana sees hail most years. Metal resists penetration but can dent from large hail. Shingles don't show dents, but they lose granules. A 2025 IBHS study found repeated hail under one inch can cause more cumulative granule loss than a single 2-inch stone, making shingles about ten times more vulnerable to the next severe storm. Impact-resistant shingles did only slightly better in that test.

Before you choose, ask your insurance agent how each material affects your premium and how cosmetic hail dents on metal are handled.

Can you put a metal roof over shingles?

Sometimes. It requires a flat, dry, sound existing roof, a metal system designed for it, and local code that allows the extra layer. Many roofers, us included, prefer a tear-off. It lets us inspect and replace bad decking, and a soft spot hidden under new metal becomes a problem later.
