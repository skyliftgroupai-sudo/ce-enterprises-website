Short answer

A kitchen remodel ranges from a refresh to a full rebuild. Nationally, a midrange minor kitchen remodel averages $28,458 and a midrange major remodel $82,793 (JLC Cost vs Value 2025). The minor remodel is the best investment, recouping about 113% of its cost at resale. Keep the layout if you can, order cabinets and countertops before demolition, and get a written scope that spells out every allowance.

The kitchen is the room people most want to change, and the one where a remodel can go over budget fastest. Here's what kitchen remodeling costs, what adds the most value, how the process works and how to choose a kitchen remodeler in Indianapolis.

Where the money goes

  • Cabinets: usually the biggest single line item
  • Countertops: quartz and stone versus laminate is a major swing
  • Layout changes: moving sinks, ranges or walls adds plumbing, electrical and drywall work
  • Appliances
  • Flooring, backsplash and lighting

Smart ways to control the budget

  1. Keep the footprint. Leaving the sink, range and fridge where they are saves on plumbing, gas and electrical.
  2. Refresh instead of replace where cabinet boxes are sound. Kitchen cabinet remodeling (new doors, drawer fronts and hardware) costs far less than new cabinets.
  3. Put your money where you touch: counters, hardware and lighting.
  4. Decide everything up front. Changes after demolition cost the most.

The kitchen remodeling process, step by step

  1. Walkthrough: how you cook, what doesn't work, budget.
  2. Written scope and estimate, with allowances clearly marked.
  3. Selections and ordering: cabinets, counters, fixtures and appliances confirmed before demolition.
  4. Demolition and rough-in: plumbing, electrical and wall changes, with permits and inspections where required.
  5. Install: cabinets, then countertop templating and install, then backsplash, flooring and fixtures.
  6. Final walkthrough and punch list.

How to choose among kitchen remodeling contractors

  • Look at finished kitchens they've done, not just stock photos
  • Get a line-item written scope with allowances you can compare
  • Ask who handles plumbing, electrical and permits
  • Ask when cabinets and counters will be ordered relative to demolition
