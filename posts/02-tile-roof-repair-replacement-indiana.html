Short answer

Most tile roof problems are fixable without replacing the tile. Clay tile can last 100+ years and concrete tile about 50, but the underlayment and flashing underneath wear out much sooner. A good tile roof repair replaces cracked or slipped tiles and fixes the flashing. A tile roof "replacement" often means removing the tiles, installing new underlayment and resetting the sound tiles. Only damaged tiles get replaced.

Tile roofs are less common in Indiana than asphalt shingles, which makes finding a tile roofing contractor harder. Many roofers simply don't work on tile. This guide explains how clay and concrete tile behave in an Indiana climate, why tile roofs leak, what tile roof repair and restoration involve, and how to pick the right tile roofing company.

Why tile roofs leak

Tile roof leak repair starts with finding the real cause. The tiles shed most of the water, but they aren't the waterproof layer. Underneath is the underlayment, and around every wall, chimney, valley and vent is flashing. That's where most tile roof leaks start:

  • Worn underlayment. It ages faster than the tile and eventually lets water through.
  • Failed flashing at walls, valleys, chimneys and skylights.
  • Cracked, chipped or slipped tiles from falling limbs, hail, foot traffic or failed fasteners.
  • Debris in valleys that dams water and pushes it sideways under the tile.
  • Freeze-thaw damage on tile that wasn't rated for a cold climate.

Tile roof repair: what's involved

  1. Inspection from a safe position. We check the tile, valleys, flashing and the attic side for staining.
  2. Lift the surrounding tiles to reach the problem without breaking more.
  3. Fix the cause: underlayment patch, new flashing, or fastener repair.
  4. Replace damaged tiles with a matching profile and color. Older or discontinued tile may need salvaged replacements.
  5. Reset and secure the tiles and clear debris from valleys.

Concrete tile roof repair and clay tile roof repair follow the same steps. The difference is matching the tile and handling it without cracking it.

Tile roof replacement vs. tile roof restoration

Tile roof restoration, meaning new underlayment with the original tiles reset, is often the best value when the tile itself is still sound.

OptionWhen it fitsWhat happens
RepairOne leak, a few broken tilesLocal fix to flashing, underlayment and tiles
Restoration / resetTiles mostly sound, underlayment worn out, leaks in several placesTiles removed and stacked, new underlayment and flashing installed, tiles relaid, broken ones replaced
Full replacementWidespread cracked or failing tile, or a change of materialEverything removed; new roof installed. Switching between tile and a lighter material needs a framing review

How to choose a tile roofing contractor

When you search "best tile roofing company" or "tile roofing contractor near me," look for:

  • Actual tile experience. Ask to see tile jobs they've done. Shingle crews can damage tile just by walking on it.
  • A plan for matching tile before they start removing anything.
  • Photos of the problem, including flashing and underlayment, not just broken tiles.
  • A written scope that says whether you're getting a repair, a reset or a replacement.
  • Freeze-thaw rated materials for any new tile in Indiana.
