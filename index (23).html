Roof repairs we handle

  • Roof leaks and ceiling stains
  • Chimney, wall and skylight flashing
  • Missing, lifted or cracked shingles
  • Pipe boot and vent leaks
  • Valley leaks
  • Roof damage from wind, hail or fallen limbs
  • Soft or rotted decking sections

Repair or replace?

If the damage is limited to one area and the rest of the roof is sound, a repair is usually the right call. If problems keep coming back in different places, we'll show you why a replacement may cost less over time. Read our repair vs. replacement guide.

Leaks that aren't the roof

Water problems are what we fix for a living. When a leak traces back to gutters, siding or a basement issue, we'll tell you — one customer called us for a roof leak and a basement leak, and we solved both.
